Tips for a Good Submission
1
Be specific about measurements, pressures, or readings observed at the time of the issue.
2
Upload a clear photo and use the annotation tool to circle or arrow the exact area of concern.
3
List what you have already tried, this saves the expert from repeating steps already done.
4
Set the correct urgency level, Critical tickets are escalated immediately.
