Where it belongs

Two answers decide the rest: which area owns it, and what kind of document it is.

A policy follows different rules

  • No area owns it. It is filed as Company Wide and binds every segment, function and center.
  • The CEO approves it, not an Operation Director.
  • It numbers as TQ-<Function>-PXXX, for example TQ-QHSE-P007 (TQ-QHSE-S001 5.3, row 02).
  • It is Internal and cannot be restricted. TQ-QHSE-S001 5.5 says policies shall be accessible to all employees.
  • Review cycle 24 months.
Open decision. BD-02 invents no CEO route; who gives a policy final approval needs QMS and business confirmation.

Who signs it

Every document is checked by QMS, then approved by the holder of its area. Maintenance documents and every Maintenance Bulletin go to the segment’s Maintenance Manager instead.

Submitter, QMS checker and final approver are three different people.

Before you file

  • Use a clear, descriptive title.
  • Make sure the document is final.
  • PDF reads best on every device.
  • One controlled document per upload.
