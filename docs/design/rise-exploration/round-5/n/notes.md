# N · Structured Technical Clarity

## Design philosophy
A serious, calm instrument for people who need the right revision fast: crisp white, cool greys, one teal for action, orange only for alerts. Every document gets an identity plate where the number and Rev are set large and status is a signal with a shape, a word and a review-cycle gauge. Facts are shown as labelled spec rows and dated timelines, never as metadata strings.

## What is better than the previous round
- Trust is something you can see: the plate (WI-003 · REV 4.0 box · green Current tile) plus a review-cycle bar (effective 30 Aug 2025 → next review 30 Aug 2028, with today marked) answers "is this current?" in about two seconds.
- A component that is clearly N's own: the stacked code column (type over a large number, e.g. SOP / 005) makes lists scannable by number, the way field users talk about documents.
- Navigation of its own: a permanent command field at the top, a 4-item bar (Home · Areas · Saved · Ask), a sticky action bar on documents; on desktop a slim rail with list and detail panes.
- Fewer explanations: "request access through QMS" appears once per screen, hidden and partial matches sit in one footer, and the proposed equipment tags and uncontrolled software are labelled with a small tag.

## Best mobile quality
The WI-003 phone screen shows identity, revision, status, approver, review cycle and the ALT-002 §5 warning in the first view, with Open PDF always one thumb-tap away.

## Best desktop quality
Search and area are true three-pane workspaces (rail · results/index · plate + preview/table), so you can scan 17 results or 8 manuals and verify the selected one without leaving the list.

## Main weakness
The tool-like rigour (hairline module heads, spec rows, tabular codes) can feel cool and a little dense next to M and O, and the home module stack on the phone is long.

## System
- **Navigation:** Phone: the command field is fixed at the top (RISE symbol · search · avatar with a notification dot that opens a sheet with the role "Viewing as Employee", Notifications, Field Glossary and Upload); bottom bar Home · Areas · Saved · Ask; documents swap the bar for an action bar (Open PDF · Saved · Share · More). Desktop: a 76px icon rail (Home, Search, Areas, Saved | Ask, Glossary, Upload | account), a 68px command bar (search with a "/" shortcut, a "Viewing as Employee" chip, a bell), then a 440px list pane (cool grey) and a detail pane (white). Master List and QMS are absent for this role.
- **Colour:** ground #FFFFFF; surfaces #F4F7F8 / #EBF0F2; hairlines #E1E7EA / #C9D3D7; ink #0E2328, secondary #44575D, tertiary #5C6E73; action TAQA Teal Green #005D63, codes in pillar teal #013F4E, teal wash #E8F3F3, cyan #00ADC2 for focus only; Current #17784C (filled circle with check), Review due #955700 (half-filled ring), Superseded #7A8A8F (ring with a slash), Alert #EE5A12 shape with #A93C05 text (triangle); orange is used for alerts only.
- **Type:** BwGradual 800 for identity numbers (plate 52/64px, Rev 34/40px, counts 26–32px) and 700 for titles (32–40 h1, 20 module, 23–26 plate title); Inter 17 body, 16.5 row titles, 16 spec values, 14 labels and meta, 13 minimum; tabular figures on every number, date and code.
- **Spacing:** 4px base; 20px phone gutter (28px list pane, 40px detail pane); modules 40px apart, each headed by a title, a count and one hairline; rows 64px minimum; touch targets 44–48px.
- **Radius:** 6 (chips, Rev marks in rows), 10 (fields, buttons, segmented control), 14 (plates, glossary term, notes); no shadows except the preview page and the active segment.
