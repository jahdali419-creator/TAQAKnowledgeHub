# M · Smooth Product Experience

## Design philosophy
A calm, carefully made app: soft tinted ground, large rounded surfaces, one clear job per region, and the rest one tap away. Trust shows up as designed objects (status capsule, approval card), not as grey sentences. Brand comes through restraint: ink type in BW Gradual, teal tints, and orange used only as the small alert marker.

## What is better than the previous round
- Each phone screen leads with one thing: a greeting and search pill, a best match, an area with its alerts, or the trust card.
- Interaction carries structure: a floating capsule tab bar, segmented controls (Saved/Recent, Documents/Areas/Experts, Operations/Maintenance, Overview/Contents/History/Related) and a floating action bar.
- Fewer lines and badges: rows hold a title plus one short line with status as word and shape. The lock is a small icon, and "request access through QMS" appears once.
- Larger type contrast (32–44px display titles, 17px body, nothing meaningful below 13px) and groups separated by space, not borders.

## Best mobile quality
The home first screen (greeting, search pill, a swipeable "For you" row with the active alert and WI‑003 to continue) feels light, finished and easy to pick up.

## Best desktop quality
A floating rounded navigation panel with one large white canvas keeps every page airy, and the document page pairs the trust card with a cover-and-actions panel.

## Main weakness
The grouped-list language is safe and close to familiar platform conventions, so the direction is memorable more for its polish than for any one signature idea.

## System
- Navigation: on the phone, a floating capsule tab bar (Home · Search · Areas · Saved). The avatar opens the profile sheet (notifications, "Viewing as Employee", Ask an Expert, Field Glossary, Upload), and Home also offers those three tools as tiles. Detail pages replace the tab bar with a floating action bar. On desktop, a floating rounded rail (four destinations plus Help and tools, with the profile at the foot) and one canvas with a search pill, the role indicator and notifications.
- Colour: ground #ECF1F1; surface #FFF (on desktop the canvas is white and groups use fill #F3F7F7); ink #002B32; text #18343B / #55696F / #7B8B90; action #005D63; deep card #013F4E; tints #E3EFEE / #EEF5F5. Status: Current #147A4E on #E1F2E8 with a check-circle; Review due #8F5600 on #FBEED5 with a clock. Orange #FD5805 is used only for the alert dot.
- Type: BW Gradual 700 for titles (phone large title 32, document 28, section 21; desktop 40–44). Inter for everything else: body 17, row title 17/500, secondary 15, capsule 14, tab label 13.
- Spacing: 16px phone gutter, 36px between phone sections (44px on desktop), rows at least 64px, touch targets 44–56px; desktop canvas padding is 56px.
- Radius: capsules 999; cards 24; groups 22; tiles 12–16; rail and canvas 28. Shadows appear only on floating elements and the For you cards.
- Proposed items: equipment chips (area) and equipment tags (document) are labelled "Proposed".
