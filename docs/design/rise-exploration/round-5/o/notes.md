# O · Refined Editorial Utility — notes

## Design philosophy
RISE reads like a calm, well-edited brief: each screen leads with one written answer set in BW Gradual, and everything else follows in a clear reading order. Documents are physical objects (designed covers with a type band and spine) so a code becomes something you recognise at a glance, and trust is said in one sentence instead of a metadata grid. Navigation gets out of the way: a slim top bar and one floating search pill at the thumb.

## What is better than the previous round
- No tab bar and no repeated row skeleton: home is a brief (lead, dated list, shelf, area), area is a contents page with dotted leaders, document is cover + trust sentence + reading index.
- Trust and change are designed objects: the ALT-002 → WI-003 §5 cover diagram, the "Current" capsule inside the trust sentence, the marked §5 in the reading index.
- Secondary facts move into disclosure, footnotes and the menu; "request access through QMS" and "2 hidden" are said once, as footnotes.
- Real typographic contrast: 34–46px display on phone, 40–48px light date numerals, 17px Inter body, 14px minimum meta.

## Best mobile quality
The first phone screen of home is one idea, beautifully set: the headline, the two covers linked by a thin orange-red rule, and one action, with search waiting at the thumb.

## Best desktop quality
A centred 1200px editorial column with a quiet masthead and an 8/4 grid, where the right column carries the personal layer (shelf, area, approvals, expert) and the left column carries the reading.

## Main weakness
Long phone scrolls on search and document rely on progressive disclosure that is drawn closed rather than interactive, and the menu sheet that carries Glossary, Ask and Upload on the phone is not drawn.

## System
- **Navigation.** Phone: top bar (RISE lockup, context crumb, menu with notification dot) + floating dark "Search RISE" pill; the pill becomes contextual ("Search Coiled Tubing", the live query with clear + Refine, or flanked by Save and More on a document). Menu sheet holds Areas, Saved, Field Glossary, Ask an Expert, Upload, Notifications, "Viewing as Employee"; home also ends with an "Everything in RISE" index. Desktop: one-row masthead (Today, Areas, Saved, Field Glossary, Ask an Expert · search field · Upload · Notifications · Employee role + avatar). No sidebar, no Master List/QMS (role-gated).
- **Colour.** Paper #F6F3ED, band #EEE9E0, raised #FCFBF8; ink #002B32 headings, text #1E3438, muted #596A6C; action TAQA Teal Green #005D63; cover bands #005D63 manual, #013F4E procedure, #2F6F78 standard, #7A6A4F form, #002B32 alert; orange→crimson gradient only as alert rule/marker (<5%). Status separate: Current #2C7A4B filled dot, Review due #946200 half ring, lock icon for Restricted/Confidential.
- **Type.** BW Gradual 400 display: 34 / 30 / 26 / 20 phone, 54 / 48 / 76 (area) desktop; BW Gradual 300 for dates and numerals (40–48); Inter 17 body, 15–16 secondary, 14 meta minimum; tabular figures for dates and counts.
- **Spacing.** 4 · 8 · 12 · 16 · 24 · 32 · 48 · 64 · 96; phone gutter 22; sections 64 apart; desktop 12-col, 40 gutter, 1200 max.
- **Radius.** Paper covers 3/7px; sheets 16–20; pills and segmented controls full; touch targets 44–56.
