From file to force

Nothing is in force until three people have signed.

1File it

Upload the file. RISE assigns the controlled number and shows who will sign.

2QMS check

QMS checks the record and the numbering, or returns it with a reason.

3Final approval

The Segment Director, or the Maintenance Manager for maintenance, approves it.

4In force

It appears in the area library. The revision it replaces is marked superseded.

Submitter, QMS checker and final approver are always three different people.
