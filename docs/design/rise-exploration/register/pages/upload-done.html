Who signs it

Every document is checked by QMS, then approved by the holder of its area. Maintenance documents and every Maintenance Bulletin go to the segment’s Maintenance Manager instead.

Submitter, QMS checker and final approver are three different people.

Before you file

  • Use a clear, descriptive title.
  • Make sure the document is final.
  • PDF reads best on every device.
  • One controlled document per upload.
