Open decisions

not fixed silently
“Your area first” in SearchThe live search has no home area (no membership yet, BR §14). Drawn and labelled proposed.
Review overdue in SearchSearch shows Employees review-overdue notes and a filter; every other page hides them. Register hides them everywhere.
Obsolete vs WithdrawnThe register says “Obsolete”; screens say “Withdrawn”. Register uses Withdrawn with the banner “Obsolete. Do not use.”
Approver wordingUpload’s success text and the record name the register’s type approver (e.g. “Relevant Operation Director”); the desk that actually signs is the area holder’s.
Policy approverUpload says the CEO approves a policy; BD-02 invents no CEO route.
Step barThe live bar shows 3 steps for 4 panels. Register shows the 4 panels.
What changed in a revisionNo change-note field is shown on the record today. Drawn as a proposed field, filled from Upload’s “What changed in this revision”.
Pins and SavedPin offline has no list. Register puts pins under Saved.
Alert / revision notificationsNot built (no subscriptions). Drawn as proposed types.
Glossary and Ask targetsBD-06 review flow and BD-04 sent state drawn as proposed; the built behaviour is drawn too.
