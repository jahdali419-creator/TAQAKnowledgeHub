About this catalogue

What RISE is, and what we need to decide

RISE is where about 5,000 TAQA employees find the current, approved version of every procedure, manual, standard and technical alert before they start a job. It is also where documents are filed, checked by QMS and approved.

The system already works. This catalogue is about how it should look and feel, so that it is trusted in the field, easy to use on a phone, and clearly a TAQA product.

We explored six directions in two rounds. Every direction shows the same real RISE content, so the comparison is about design, not data.

520documents in force
25areas across the company
33active technical alerts
~5,000employees, field and office

Round 1 · A, B, C

Desktop-first, premium product directions. Feedback: too dense for a phone, and the RISE brand should stand out more.

Round 2 · D, E, F

Designed for the phone first, with fewer elements per screen and a strong, distinctive RISE brand moment. Each then scales up to the desktop.

Nothing in the live RISE system has been changed. All existing functions and business rules (approvals, QMS checks, separation of duties, Arabic translations) are kept in every direction.

Round 1 · A · Instrument

A

Instrument

A precise work tool. The document register is the hero: a calm app with a side menu, dense tables and the same layout for every role.

Why it works

  • The fastest way to scan many documents and check their status.
  • Handles the heaviest work (QMS checks, approvals, audits) without new screens.
  • Every role sees the same layout, so training is simple.

Watch out for

  • The quietest brand of the six: little emotion, no photography.
  • Heavier on the phone than the round 2 directions.

Round 1 was designed for the desktop first. Feedback on this round led to directions D, E and F.

Round 1 · B · Flagship

B

Flagship

RISE as TAQA's flagship product: WHAT WE KNOW. set large, deep ink bands and real TAQA field photography, like a premium annual report.

Why it works

  • The strongest sense of pride and the most premium first impression.
  • One big idea per screen, so pages are easy to read.
  • Uses the logo's own colours and shapes throughout.

Watch out for

  • Shows fewer documents per screen.
  • Could drift towards a marketing website if the style spreads.

Round 1 · C · Atlas

C

Atlas

A reference library. Every document has a place, a code and a history, presented with the clarity of a technical publisher.

Why it works

  • The best reading experience and the clearest proof that a document is current.
  • The approval history reads like a family tree, which builds trust.
  • Teaches people the document numbering system as they use it.

Watch out for

  • A quieter brand moment; it relies on typography.
  • Less dense than Instrument for heavy QMS work.

Round 2 · D · Beacon

D

Beacon

RISE as a lit signal. The deep ink of the logo is the background, and the logo's own colours are the only light. The logo assembles on screen when the app opens.

Why it works

  • The strongest first impression of the six.
  • The phone home shows only three things: search, the critical alert and the last document opened.
  • Calm and very legible in control cabins and on night shifts.

Watch out for

  • Dark screens are harder to read in direct sunlight on site.
  • Long procedures switch to a light page for reading.

Brand moment · The logo assembles in 0.7 seconds, once per session

Round 2 · E · Full Stop

E

Full Stop

Typography does the work. One typeface on warm paper, and the orange full stop from WHAT WE KNOW. is the only accent in the whole product.

Why it works

  • The calmest, most readable screens of the six.
  • The orange dot marks what matters: the active tab, alerts, new notifications.
  • Trust is written in plain sentences: "Current. Approved 28 Aug 2025 by Segment Director, Coiled Tubing."

Watch out for

  • The brand depends on the TAQA typeface loading correctly.
  • Some may find the first screen too empty.

Brand moment · WHAT WE KNOW. builds on the home screen, no splash screen

Round 2 · F · Rise

F

Rise

The logo becomes the interface. The teal pillar is the header (a side rail on desktop), the orange and cyan bars become colour tiles, and every tile carries the logo's 45° cut.

Why it works

  • The most recognisable brand of the six, on every screen.
  • Big, glove-friendly buttons for field use.
  • Each colour has one job: orange means alert, cyan means action.

Watch out for

  • Colour blocks are loud; the "orange means alert" rule must be strict.
  • Tiles show fewer items, so busy screens switch to lists.

Brand moment · The screen rises into the logo in 0.7 seconds, once per session

Decision

What we are asking for

Choose one direction to take forward, or a combination of two (for example the calm typography of Full Stop with the opening moment of Beacon).

A simple way to choose

  • If field use on the phone comes firstRise (F) or Full Stop (E)
  • If brand pride and first impression come firstBeacon (D) or Flagship (B)
  • If reading and trust in documents come firstFull Stop (E) or Atlas (C)
  • If heavy QMS and audit work comes firstInstrument (A)

What happens next

  1. Complete the chosen designThe remaining screens: Director approvals, Master Document List, the full filing steps and every document status.
  2. Agree an implementation planScope, timing and testing, presented for approval before any build starts.
  3. Build and test with field usersOn real phones, on site, before release.

Until a direction and a plan are approved, nothing changes in the live RISE system.
