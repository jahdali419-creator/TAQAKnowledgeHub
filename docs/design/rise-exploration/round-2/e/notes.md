# Option E · Full Stop

**Idea.** Type does the work. One typeface (BW Gradual) on warm paper, with the orange full stop from WHAT WE KNOW. as the only accent in the whole product.

**Brand moment.** There is no splash screen. When Home opens, WHAT, WE and KNOW rise into place 70 ms apart (420 ms each) and the full stop drops in at 380 ms, for 600 ms in total, once per session. The statement is the home screen itself, so nothing waits. BW Gradual's full stop is square, so the round dot is drawn (`.fs`) to match the logo.

**The full stop as a system.** It marks the end of the statement and area titles ("Coiled Tubing."), the active tab (a dot under the word), unread notifications and critical alerts. It never appears on document titles, which are data.

**Why it works**
- These are the quietest screens of the three: no cards, no tiles and no icons in the tab bar. Status is a word plus a shape: a dot for Current and a diamond for Review due.
- Search sits in the thumb zone at the bottom of the first screen, just under the one critical alert.
- Trust is written as a sentence: "Current. Rev 4.0, Internal. Approved 28 Aug 2025 by Segment Director, Coiled Tubing."
- The search screen offers Ask an Expert, with the question filled in, when the results aren't enough.

**Watch out for**
- The brand depends on BW Gradual loading. It is weak with a fallback font.
- The first screen has the most open space of the three, and the logo colours appear only in the lockup.

**Tokens.** Paper `#F5F3EE` · ink `#002B32` · ink-2 `#3F5559` · ink-3 `#5E6F72` · rule `#DDD8CE` · teal `#005D63` (links) · orange `#FD5805` (the full stop only, never text) · Current `#1A7547` · Review due `#8A5800` with a `#D99500` mark. Type: BW Gradual 800 for the statement (92 px on the phone, 158 px on desktop), 700 for titles, 400 for reading at 17–18 px. Inter only for document numbers and dates (tabular figures). Square-cornered controls (4 px).
