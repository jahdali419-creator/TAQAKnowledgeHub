Round 6 · One new direction

Halo

Every document wears its trust.

A deep TAQA-ink hero with a seismic texture, a light work sheet that rises over it, and one signature object: the trust seal. Its ring of month-ticks shows exactly where a document is in its review cycle, and its rim carries the document’s full identity. Home leads with the one change that touches your work today.

The signature

The trust seal

Today, “is this the right revision, and is it still trusted?” is answered by reading grey metadata. Halo turns the answer into one object you recognise from across a room.

  1. 1Engraved rimThe full number, title, area and side, written around the seal. You can screenshot it and it still says exactly which document it is.
  2. 236 month-ticksOne tick for every month of WI-003’s 36-month review cycle. Lit ticks have passed: 13 of 36 since it took effect on 30 Aug 2025.
  3. 3This monthThe single orange spark, the only orange on the screen. Next review is 30 Aug 2028, when the ring closes.
  4. 4The centreNumber, revision and status, readable in two seconds from arm’s length.

How it was made

Five design skills, one result

TAQA brand + TAQA UI/UXTeal Green #00585A, Legacy Teal #00BFB2 for every lit tick, orange only as a single spark, Crimson safety callout for ALT-002, BW Gradual everywhere, filled two-tone icons, the official RISE logo files.
TAQA KnowledgeHub frontendThe surface scale (#F4F6F6 sheet, white cards), teal-tinted shadows, 100dvh layouts, skip link, visible focus rings, role indicator, and logical structure ready for Arabic.
Design tasteAn asymmetric split hero instead of a centred one, dark and light materials with a real reason (the hero is identity, the sheet is work), inner-edge glass on the dock, and no generic card grids.
Motion polishEase-out curves, every tap under 250 ms, presses scale to 0.97–0.98, entrances play once and never loop. The seal’s ticks sweep in, the rim settles, the figures count up. Reduced-motion users get none of it.
Data visualisationThe shelf bar uses Coiled Tubing’s real counts (15 SOPs, 8 manuals, 5 standards, 4 lessons, 2 forms, 2 alerts = 36) with every value written next to its colour, so colour is never the only signal.

Honest notes

Before you fall in love

Sunlight. A deep hero is beautiful indoors and harder in direct sun. The live app already has a Sunlight mode for the quick reference card; Halo would need a light variant of the hero.

Every seal needs a review cycle. The seal is only honest if each document type has a known review period. Where it is missing, the seal should show the status without ticks.

It is one direction. Halo is shown on its own so you can judge it. N, N + TAQA and the earlier rounds are unchanged, and nothing is chosen or built.
