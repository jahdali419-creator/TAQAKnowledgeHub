## Idea
A deep TAQA-ink hero (#002B32) with a seismic-trace texture, and a light work sheet (#F4F6F6) rising over it.
Signature: the trust seal. 36 month-ticks of the review cycle (lit = elapsed, one orange spark = this month), the
full identity engraved on the rim, number, revision and status in the centre. Home leads with the one change that
touches the viewer's work (ALT-002 → WI-003 §5).
