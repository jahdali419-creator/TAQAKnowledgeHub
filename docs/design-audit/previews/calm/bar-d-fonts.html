Option D — the font, the size and the grey

The pill is identical in every example below: 44px tall, fully round, #F7F6F4 fill, no border. Only the type changes, so what you are judging is type and nothing else.

The font in the bar today is Inter. It is used for every control and every line of body text on the site. Bw Gradual is the TAQA display face and is used only for headings — "Search", the big stat numbers, segment titles.

Three families are already installed and self-hosted in fonts/web/: Bw Gradual (4 weights), Inter (300–700) and Urbanist (300–800). Urbanist is currently only a fallback behind Bw Gradual, so it is paid for and unused. Picking any of the three costs nothing extra to download — which matters, because every font file also sits in the service-worker cache for offline use on a rig.

1 · Which font

All four at the same 13.5px so the difference you see is the typeface, not the size. Note that Urbanist has a shorter x-height than Inter, so it reads a little smaller at the same number.

Inter — today

Built for screens. Tall x-height, wide apertures, unmistakable at 13px. The safe, neutral choice.

Urbanist — already shipped

Geometric and rounder. The circular letterforms echo the pill shape, so D looks deliberate rather than just shortened.

Bw Gradual — the brand face

Most on-brand and the least suited to a form field: it is a display face, drawn for headlines, and it loses clarity at small sizes.

System — no download

Whatever the device uses. Fastest and never a flash of the wrong font, but it looks different on every machine.

3 · How grey

Measured against the #F7F6F4 pill, not guessed. WCAG AA wants 4.5:1 for text this size.

#5F564F — 6.63:1

Comfortable pass. Reads as dark grey rather than a hint.

#6E655D — 5.28:1

Passes with room to spare and still reads as a prompt. The lightest I would sign off.

#7D746C — 4.24:1

Just under AA. Fine in an office, marginal in sunlight through a visor.

#8A817A — 3.53:1

Clearly fails. Looks elegant on a desk and disappears on a tablet outdoors.
