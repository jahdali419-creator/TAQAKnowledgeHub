1The incidentRun 3 · 7,480 ft

On the third run of a fishing job the string lost returns at surface and pump pressure fell away over roughly four minutes. The string was pulled and a washout was found in the washpipe immediately above the connection. No injury, no release to the environment, 6.5 hours of non-productive time.

2What was happening

Fishing for a dropped gauge carrier after two earlier runs. Nitrogen was being injected to assist returns. The string had 118 running hours and was 11 runs into the interval between inspections.

3Symptoms, in order4 entries

  1. +0 minStandpipe pressure dropped 380 psi with no change to pump rate.
  2. +2 minReturns at surface became intermittent, then stopped.
  3. +4 minWeight indicator showed 1,100 lb less than the previous run at the same depth.
  4. +9 minPumping stopped, string pulled at reduced speed.

4What was tried

ActionResultNote
Reduced pump rate to 0.8 bbl/minPartialPressure steadied briefly, then fell again.
Stopped nitrogen, pumped water onlyFailedNo change to returns.
Pulled out of hole at 15 ft/minWorkedString recovered intact; washout found on inspection.

5How it ended

Pulled out of hole. The washpipe section was cut out and sent for examination. The fish was recovered on the following run with a replacement string. 6.5 hours NPT.
