About this platform

What the document control layer does, what the back end has to supply, and what is still open with QHSE.

About the data. Titles for the twelve original segments are real; newer areas carry representative ones. Revision, approval and status values are generated so the controls can be seen working. Every figure here is counted from the register.

1

A register, where there was none

TQ-QHSE-S001 5.1 requires a Master Document List. The Hub held its documents in three copies that nothing kept in step.

  • The register Filter by area, type, status, class or language. Every view is a link you can send.
  • Waiting on a short form These cannot take a conformant number under TQ-QHSE-S001 5.3 until QHSE assigns one.
  • More than six months overdue Of reviews past their date, are more than 180 days late.
2

Documents carry real control metadata

The viewer used to derive revision, issue date and author by hashing the title against a list of six names. It now reads the register, and says plainly when a value is missing.

3

Numbering follows TQ-QHSE-S001 5.3

The Hub was inventing its own scheme, and two of its prefixes collided with short forms the standard had already assigned elsewhere.

WasNowWhy it mattered
CT-001toTQ-TWS-CTSS-SOP-001Business unit, service line and type are all carried in the number.
WS-001toTQ-TWS-WSS-SOP-001Collision. The Hub used WS for Well Safety. The standard assigns WS to Wireline Services.
CS-PROC-001toTQ-GRC-SOP-001Collision. The Hub used CS for Cybersecurity. The standard assigns CS to Customer Service, and the entity is now Governance, Risk & Compliance.
ALERT-CT-001toTQ-TWS-CTSS-ALT-001Technical Alert is not a type defined in section 4, so no conformant code exists.
4

Who can see and control what

Role decides which functions you get. Classification decides which documents. Every role can still confirm the current revision of a document before a job, through Search and the area libraries, because TQ-QHSE-S001 5.5 requires it.

RoleSeesControls
  • Try it: switch role Use the View as switch on the top bar; on a phone it sits under Viewing as, at the foot of the menu. As Employee, the Master List, this page and the export close, and documents drop out of view.
  • A specification, not a security control Role switching runs in the browser and proves nothing. The back end applies the same rules on the server, from an Entra ID group through the SSO claim to a check on every record.
