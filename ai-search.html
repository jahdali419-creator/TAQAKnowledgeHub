How Search works. It runs in this browser over the register and the field cases your role may open; nothing you type leaves the device. A document matches when every word appears in its title, number, area or type; a field case matches when every word appears anywhere in it. Withdrawn documents are left out, except that typing the exact number of one says what happened to it.
